On this page

    1

    Introduction

    Welcome to Wonderly, an Augmented Reality (AR) educational application developed by Dwit Labs.

    Wonderly provides interactive educational experiences for preschool children, including alphabets, numbers, shapes, and colors. We respect the privacy of children and parents and are committed to protecting user information.

    2

    Information We Collect

    Wonderly does not require users to create an account or provide their name, email address, or phone number to use its core learning features.

    However, certain information may be processed through third-party services integrated into the application, including advertising services.

    3

    Camera and AR Permissions

    Wonderly uses the device camera to provide Augmented Reality functionality. Camera access is required to:

    • Detect surfaces in the user's environment.
    • Place virtual classroom objects.
    • Display interactive 3D educational content.
    Good to knowCamera images and video are not stored or transmitted by Wonderly, provided this remains consistent with the application's implementation and SDK behavior.

    Camera access is used for AR functionality. Users can disable camera permission through their device settings, although AR functionality may no longer work.

    4

    Advertising

    Wonderly may display advertisements through Google AdMob.

    Third-party advertising services may process certain information, such as device information, advertising identifiers where permitted, and ad interaction data, according to their applicable policies.

    For child-directed users, we configure advertising in accordance with applicable Google Play Families requirements, including restrictions on personalized advertising.

    Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy

    5

    Children's Privacy

    Wonderly is designed for preschool children and their parents.

    We do not intentionally request children's names, email addresses, phone numbers, or precise location information.

    We aim to comply with applicable children's privacy laws and Google Play Families policies.

    Parents or guardians may contact us with questions or requests relating to privacy.
